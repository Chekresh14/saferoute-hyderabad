Prototype, not an emergency service

No area is labelled safe or unsafe by this page. Map coverage, route availability, and data completeness can vary. In an emergency in India, call 112.

How to make it real

1. Obtain official, dated Hyderabad datasets.
2. Standardize locations and remove duplicates.
3. Publish methodology and uncertainty.
4. Validate with local experts and community groups.
5. Update sources regularly and protect reporter privacy.
